Whole-Home & Multi-Room Remodeling

One contractor for the kitchen, the bathrooms, the floors and the finish work, in Coeur d'Alene, Post Falls and Hayden.

Most remodels don't stop at one room. New flooring runs into the next room, a kitchen update makes the bathroom look dated, and the trim has to tie it all together. When that happens, hiring a separate company for every part means more schedules to juggle and more chances for the work not to match.

DCB Construction LLC handles the interior trades of a remodel under one roof, so several rooms can be planned, priced and scheduled as a single project.

What a Multi-Room Remodel Can Include

  • Kitchen remodeling: layout, cabinetry, countertops and backsplash.
  • Bathroom remodeling: full bathroom updates, from waterproofing to fixtures.
  • Walk-in and curbless showers: custom tile showers with niches, benches and glass.
  • Tile installation: floors, walls and backsplashes.
  • Fireplace tile: surrounds, hearths and feature walls.
  • Flooring: new floors that carry from room to room.
  • In-floor heating: radiant heat under tile, added while the floor is already open.
  • Trim and finish carpentry: baseboards, casing and crown that match throughout the house.

Why Plan Rooms Together

  • Everything matches. Tile, flooring and trim are chosen together rather than fitted around what was done last time.
  • Work happens in the right order. Floors go in before trim, and heat goes in before tile, so nothing gets torn out twice.
  • One point of contact. You have one company to call and one schedule to follow.
  • One clear estimate. You see the whole project before you commit, and you can decide which rooms to do now and which to leave for later.

Serving North Idaho Homes

We work with homeowners in Coeur d'Alene, Post Falls and Hayden, and across Kootenai County. Tell us what you have in mind, even if it's only a list of rooms, and we'll help you work out a scope and an order that fit your budget.
